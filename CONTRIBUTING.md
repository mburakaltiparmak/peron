# Contributing to Peron

Thanks for your interest in Peron.

- **Bugs and ideas:** open an issue, or use the Contact tab in the app.
- **Security issues:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); please don't open a public issue.
- **Pull requests:** welcome. Before one can be merged:
  - you agree to the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) (tick the box in the pull request
    template). You keep your copyright; the CLA lets the maintainer ship your change in the
    official Microsoft Store build as well as under the GPL-3.0;
  - the maintainer reviews and approves it. Nothing is merged into `main` without that approval,
    and a pull request may be closed without merging. Workflows from forks run only after the
    maintainer approves them.
- **Forks** are welcome under the [GPL-3.0](LICENSE). The name "Peron" and its logo are not
  covered by the GPL: publish a modified version under a different name and without the logo.
